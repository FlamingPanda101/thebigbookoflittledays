VERDICT
==============================================================================
Days 1-182 are not ready to go to design as they stand, but the gap is narrower than the length of this list suggests. Two classes of defect are blocking. First, safety: the book sets an excellent too-big-to-swallow standard and then breaks it itself on roughly fifteen days, handing a 6-to-12-month-old ice cubes, cotton wool balls, a carrier bag, untaped rattle lids, straws, foil balls, balloons and whole grapes and cherry tomatoes, and leaving five water days without the arm's-reach sentence it uses correctly everywhere else. None of these is a hard fix (almost every one has a correct model on another page of the same book), but they cannot ship, and they are spread across enough pages that fixing them after layout means re-flowing text. Second, the country: the book runs a British plant calendar, British groceries and British coins underneath American holidays. Two auditors found this independently from opposite ends. It is the single largest edit in the file, it touches forty-odd days, and it must be decided before anything is set, because both the fix and the page count change with the answer. Beyond those two, the content itself is strong: the safety writing is specific and action-bearing on the great majority of days, the infant arc genuinely develops, the phenology is unusually well observed, and the voice does not preach. The remaining problems are real but ordinary book problems - about nine pairs of days that run the same activity from the same shopping list, one tip in eight saying do not correct her, and a research-sentence habit that overclaims in a consistent direction. Fix the safety sweep and settle the country, and this goes to design as a good book with a known rewrite queue behind it.

STRUCK: 8 findings
Struck as taste rather than defect: (1) the 'The [noun] That [verb]' section-title formula on ~35 sections - it is a house voice, not an error; (2) the '...She [verb]s Herself' title formula and the proposed cap of three; (3) the 'cut/move it more than feels right' tip template across nine days; (4) 'see whether' closing eighteen outdoor sections; (5) the 'Everywhere' naming convention on nine Around the World features - the naming is struck, though the D169/D176 subject overlap is kept as a real duplicate; (6) D75's single first-person authorial aside against D42's third person - a good line, and one deliberate aside is not an editing miss; (7) the D76/D143 'leave it standing' versus 'give it a last moment' tension, which is two different objects and not a contradiction; (8) D160/D71/D118 missing quantities was folded into a single materials line rather than standing as three findings. Nothing was struck as out-of-brief structural, because no auditor reported structure.

MATERIALS: The recycling-and-cupboard promise holds on the large majority of days and the substitution habit is genuinely good. Three things break it: about twenty-one days that silently need work done the night before, a handful of items that are unbuyable or uncountable (100 bottle caps with no save-up notice, fifty eggs across twelve days, newspaper by the stack, a compulsory paddling pool with no substitute), and the British shopping vocabulary sitting inside an American calendar. REPETITION: The weakest dimension and the one most visible to a reader working the book straight through. Separately, about one tip in eight is 'do not correct her' in a different sentence, which matters because the tip line is also where the load-bearing warnings live. None of this is dangerous; all of it is rewrite work that should happen before text is set.

INSIGHTS: The voice is the best thing in the book - competent-adult register, expectations set downward, almost no preaching. Three problems: one insight with a physical-risk edge (D78 removes the adult brake on spinning), one that plants a screening question with no exit (D91), and a research-sentence habit that overclaims in a consistent direction, most visibly the object-permanence error on D51 and three claims that rank an underdog skill above the conventional one when the evidence runs the other way. The eleven days that assert Azlyn held an activity for forty minutes to an hour are the quiet defect here: they are the number Brooklyn measures herself against.

INFANT: The developmental arc is well built and Kreston becomes a real participant by Day 156 - this is a strength, not a filler thread. But the mouthing-safety layer fails at roughly a dozen points where the book hands a baby an object that violates its own too-big-to-swallow rule, and the water language goes soft on three swim days and one bucket. The February-to-April middle also runs on a small loop: thirteen near-identical library afternoons and twenty minutes as the answer to everything from a supermarket at 7 months to a botanical garden at 12.

SEASON: The month-by-month phenology is unusually well observed and not one Get Outside is a generic park-trip entry - this is the dimension with the most craft in it.

  GET OUTSIDE: Go out hunting for the largest version of ordinary things: the tallest tree on the block, the biggest truck parked up, a flattened refrigerator box at the curb, the dog that comes up to her shoulder. D361 (Mon Dec 27) The Candle Rules
  GET OUTSIDE: The sun stays low all day this week, so her shadow runs long at eleven in the morning. Find pale sidewalk in full sun, stand her at one end, and pace out her shadow in your feet, then hers. Turn her to face the sun and watch it swap ends. <div style="page-break-after: always;"></div>

D362 (Tue Dec 28) The Print It Leaves Behind
  GET OUTSIDE: Morning frost sits on grass, car roofs and every shaded patch the low sun misses. Walk her across a white lawn and turn round to look at her boot trail. Everything out here loses its print by lunchtime, and the ten on the counter are not going anywhere. <div style="page-break-after: always;"></div>

  GET OUTSIDE: Half the street has left its lights on in daylight this week and they look washed out at eleven in the morning.

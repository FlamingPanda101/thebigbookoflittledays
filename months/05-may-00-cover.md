## ⚠️ Quick Safety Reminders

- **Small parts**: the button tin on Day 130, the tray of things smaller than her thumb on Day 137, the pasta shapes on Day 138, the loose oats and raisins on Day 150. Kreston pulls to stand at table height, so work high and count everything back into its box before he is on the floor.
- **String, wool and cord**: the lifting machine on Day 142, the seven wool medal loops on Day 143 and the camera strap on Day 127 all use them. Nothing goes around a neck, Kreston's medal tapes flat to his high-chair tray, and the string comes down the moment the activity ends.
- **Standing water**: the bubble tray on Day 134, the bucket and Kreston's pouring tray on Day 139, the dishpan flood on Day 142, the pool on the four swimming afternoons. Kreston can drown in a few inches of water and he does it in silence, so tip every container out when you finish.
- **Roadside plants are up now**: poison hemlock on the Day 129, Day 151 and Day 153 walks, waist-high, lacy white heads on a purple-blotched stem, and wild parsnip on the Day 129 walk, flat yellow heads and sap that burns skin in sunlight. Pick nothing tall, white and lacy on any walk this month, and wash both pairs of hands before anybody eats.
- **Adult-only tools**: the pan of hot oil on Day 131, the screwdriver on Day 136, the heavy scissors on Day 141, the craft knife on Day 148. Day 136 also opens a dead flashlight, so the batteries go into a sealed bag on a high shelf first, because a swallowed button battery burns a child from the inside within hours.
- **The darning needle on Day 131**: she pulls it through from her side while you push it from yours, so it stays hand to hand and never on the table. It goes back in the tin the second you stop.
- **Late spring sun**: the foil oven and its metal tray run hot on Day 135, and Day 137's glass magnifying lens focuses sunlight hot enough to scorch paper and skin, so the lens stays indoors on the table with you. Sunscreen before you go out and again after lunch, hats, shade, water, and check the back of Kreston's neck because he overheats before he can say so.

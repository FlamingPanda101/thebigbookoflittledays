## ⚠️ Quick Safety Reminders

- **Small parts** — dried beans, pasta, lolly sticks, salt dough bricks. A choking risk for *both* children. Count them out and count them back in.
- **String and cord** — the zip line and the wrecking ball both use it. Take it down the moment the activity ends. Never leave a hanging loop within reach.
- **Adult-only steps** — craft knives, scissors, the oven. Marked in each activity.
- **Salt dough is not food.** It is dangerous if eaten, especially for Kreston and for any dog in the house.
- **Winter specific** — check for ice before outdoor play, and keep outings short below freezing. Little bodies lose heat fast.

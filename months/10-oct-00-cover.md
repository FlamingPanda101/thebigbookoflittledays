## ⚠️ Quick Safety Reminders

- **Small parts**: the counting jar on Days 274 to 276, 279 and 280, the marbles on Day 289, the card coins on Day 290, and the tray of gravel and shells on Day 300. Every one of them fits Kreston's airway, so count them out, count them back in, and send the jar up high the moment a round ends.
- **String and cord**: the card line on Day 280, the balloon rocket line on Day 290, the parachute lines on Day 294, and the gallery strings on Days 307 and 308. Take it down the moment the activity ends, because a line at toddler height is a strangulation risk.
- **Adult-only steps**: the craft knife on Days 281, 288 and 303, the pumpkin knife on Day 304, the stapler on Days 286 and 295, the hot pan on Day 293, the oven on Day 302. Look for the adult-only mark in each activity, and the knife goes back on a high shelf between cuts.
- **Balloons and plastic bags**: the balloon rockets on Day 290, the freezer bags on Day 293, the grocery bags cut for parachutes on Day 294. A burst balloon and a bag over the face both take seconds, so count the balloons in and out and keep every scrap and spare bag in your pocket.
- **Things that look like food and are not**: the salt dough on Days 296 and 302, the spring bulbs on Day 292, the neat food coloring on Day 302, the ivy and holly on Day 307. Each one does real harm swallowed, so they stay at your end of the table and both children wash their hands before they leave the room.
- **Late October specific**: three lit festivals land in four days, Diwali on Day 302, Halloween on Day 304 and Día de los Muertos on Day 305, so use battery tealights for all three, and on Halloween night Azlyn's hand stays in yours at every curb while Kreston rides in the stroller or the carrier. Drowsy wasps sit on the ground she kneels on during the Day 295 bug hunt and cluster on the fallen apples on Day 298, so keep shoes on both children and turn the apples with a stick.

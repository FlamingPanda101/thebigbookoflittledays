## ⚠️ Quick Safety Reminders

- **Small parts**: the whistle and the pea inside it, ten bottle caps at the flicking game, six paperclips on the paper planes, the bottle-cap coins at the market stall, the rice and pasta in the guessing tubs. Count them out and count them back in, then stand the tin up high.
- **Standing water**: the bottle canal and the paddling-pool lagoon, the duck-hooking bowl, his own inch in a baking tray. Kreston drowns in an inch of it without a sound, so stay within arm's reach and tip every container onto the grass the moment you stop using it.
- **At head height**: hang the bunting line across the yard high and tight, and take it and the string across the museum table down the same day. Tape the broom-handle gate barrier at one end only, because the far end has to drop when Kreston leans his whole weight on it.
- **Button batteries**: the flashlight dancing and the sound hunt both run on them, and they are the worst thing a 14-month-old can swallow. Check the battery door on every flashlight before he is in the room, and never leave a loose one on the floor.
- **Adult-only steps**: the craft knife for the ticket press, the sharp knife for the oranges and the bottle channels, the stapler and the table knife for the passport, the dry pan for the tortillas. Look for the adult-only mark in each activity, and the scissors stay with you.
- **August heat**: hat on, cream at eight, cream again at eleven, every day in this booklet. Paving burns bare feet by mid-morning, so shoes on for the chalk work, shade for anything past noon, and both children indoors the moment either goes red-faced.

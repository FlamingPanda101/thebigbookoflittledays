## ⚠️ Quick Safety Reminders

- **Small parts, and his hand works on all of them now**: beans, stones, dry pasta, torn paper strips, snails, the bottle caps and buttons on the 27th. Count them out at table height and count them back into the tin, and use a flat fridge magnet for the test on the 25th, because a small strong one means surgery.
- **Standing water**: the pouring buckets on the 10th, the dam tray on the 19th, the float-test dishpan on the 25th, the pulp bowl on the 26th, swimming every Tuesday. Two inches drown him, so tip every container out as you step away and stay in arm's reach at the pool.
- **String and cord**: the coat hanger scale on the 10th, the blind cords by the window chair on the 16th, the wall strings on the 17th, the wild corner boundary line on the 22nd, the truck pull cord on the 24th. Take every length down as the activity ends, and tie the blind cords up out of reach before she climbs.
- **Everything sitting at his new height**: four mugs of dye water on the 7th, the celery jars on the 11th, the cardboard hide on the 21st, the perfume jars on the 30th, the soil-filled glass jar on May 6. Push the wet things past the middle of the table, and tape the hide to the chair legs.
- **Adult-only steps**: the stovetop on the 2nd and the 8th, the kitchen knife on the 8th, the craft knife on every cut bottle (rain gauge, rot jars, filter, worm tower) and on the cardboard hide, the adult scissors most days, the broken glass and cans on the 22nd. Tape every cut rim before either child touches it, and keep Kreston out of the kitchen while a pan is on the stove.
- **Spring specific**: raw egg through Week 14, soil, compost and worm bedding all month, daffodil sap, foxglove and lily in her hands on the 30th, wasps at the butterfly plate on May 1, ticks by May 5. Scrub both children before food and before anyone lifts Kreston, keep every flower out of both mouths, and check hairlines, necks and behind the knees when you come in.

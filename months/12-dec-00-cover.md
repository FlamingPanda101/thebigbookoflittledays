## ⚠️ Quick Safety Reminders

- **Real flame**: the menorah on the 24th, the ice lantern on the 21st, the candle rules on the 27th, her cake on the 20th and your own cake on the 29th. Stay in the room while any of them burn, and the matches go back on a high shelf after every strike.
- **Button batteries**: the tea lights in the lit village on the 9th, and the one on the memory table on the 30th. Tape every cover shut and store them high, because a button battery swallowed at 18 months is a surgical emergency.
- **Small parts**: cork and clothespin villagers on the 4th, bottle caps on the 6th, birdseed and chopped nuts on the 10th, salt dough rounds on the 16th, foil coins on the 24th, hard candy on the 29th. Count them out, count them back into a box, and sweep the floor before Kreston goes down.
- **String and cord**: the bird feeders on the 10th, the name tags on the 11th and the paper cloud on the 18th all hang on it. Nothing stays looped within his reach, and the indoor ones come down at night.
- **Adult-only steps**: the craft knife on the 3rd, the 7th, the 9th, the 15th and the 26th, the kitchen scissors on the 26th and the 28th, melted fat on the 10th, the oven on the 16th and the 20th, the stove and the heated spoon on the 27th. Christmas cardboard hides staples in the seams, so run a hand down every panel first.
- **Winter specific**: the dark walks put them at the curb by half past three, so hold both their hands and stay off the road edge. Pond ice never holds a child, nothing red comes home off the collecting walks, and Kreston can drown in the inch of water in the river tray on the 23rd.

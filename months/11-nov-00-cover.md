## ⚠️ Quick Safety Reminders

- **Small parts**: cotton balls on shearing day, loose rice and lentils, the pinwheel fasteners, twenty-nine countdown pebbles, oats and dried pasta. Kreston is on his feet all month and helps himself, so count them out and count them back in.
- **Hot liquid and the oven**: the milk pan for curds and whey on the 10th, the Thanksgiving oven on the 25th, boiling noodle water on the 27th, the muffins on the 29th, the cheese straws on December 1, and the dinner Azlyn cooks on December 2. Turn every handle inward and keep Kreston gated while pans move.
- **Adult-only tools**: the pin for the milking glove on the 6th, the knife for the squash on the 19th, the box grater, and the scissors in most of the crafts. Three of them carry the adult-only mark, and the grater stays in your hands while she pushes the cheese.
- **String, wool and plastic bags**: the wool ear tags on the 9th tie round animal legs, and the windsock tails on the 13th and the leaf bags on the 21st are plastic. Tie every tag short and put spare bags away before either child comes near them.
- **Standing water**: the milking bucket on the 6th, the tray under the mirror on the 15th, and swimming every Tuesday. Empty the bucket and the tray the moment the activity stops, and keep both hands on Kreston in the pool.
- **November specific**: mud, the first frost, dark by four thirty, and the evergreen hunt on the 20th that walks past yew and holly. Take the outdoor block in the morning light, and sort the greenery bag yourself before it reaches Kreston.

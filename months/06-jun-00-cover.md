## ⚠️ Quick Safety Reminders

- **Standing water**: the dishpans of color on Day 160, the kiddie pool through all of Week 24, the tub under the sheet on Day 178. Kreston climbs into a pool at one year and cannot climb back out, so tip every container out when you finish.
- **Small round things**: the bottle caps on Day 162, the beans on Day 167, the map markers on Day 171, the rice in the shaker on Day 176. Each one matches Kreston's airway at twelve months, and you quarter Day 170's cherry tomatoes lengthwise before they reach his tray.
- **Balloons**: three come out for the smash cake on Day 156 and two more for the static tests on Day 161. You inflate them, they stay above head height, and you pick a burst one up whole before he is back on the floor.
- **String and cord**: the wind line on Day 161, the throw line on Day 165, the family line on Day 170, the bunting on Day 181. All of it goes up out of reach or comes down when the activity ends, because a loop at Kreston's height is a strangulation risk.
- **Adult-only steps**: the oven and the candle on Day 156, the table knife on Day 170, the scissors on most days from Day 155 on. "The scissors stay with you" runs through the month, because he is on his feet now and a table edge sits at his eye level.
- **Late June heat**: hats and sunscreen on both of them before you open the door, then again after any water. Put your own hand flat on paving, dark plastic and metal first, and shade the stroller by parking it rather than draping a cloth over the top, which traps the heat.

## ⚠️ Quick Safety Reminders

- **Small parts**: buttons at the bakery counter on Day 39, dyed pasta on Day 46, stamps and coins on Day 48, lentils and beans on Day 52, pasta and cut straws on Day 54, crayon stubs and keys on Day 55. Sweep before Kreston is down, count them out and count them back in, and treat Day 40's balloons as the worst of the list.
- **String and cord**: the floss that cuts the buns on Day 42, the heart garland on Day 45, the pasta necklaces on Days 46 and 54, the gallery line on Day 56, the string telephone on Day 62. The hung ones come down the moment the activity ends, and no cord or beaded necklace goes on a baby.
- **Adult-only steps**: the oven runs on five days and hits 475°F for the pizzas on Day 38, and Day 63 simmers a pan of orange peel for an hour. Hot trays come out at Kreston's head height, so block his route before one goes in, and the craft knife on Days 43 and 51 goes back up between cuts.
- **Salt dough and playdough are not food.** The month mixes them three times, on Days 39, 52 and 61, and both carry far more salt than anything on her plate. Kreston's piece goes in a small rigid pot with the lid taped on hard, never in a food bag.
- **Standing water**: the dishpans on the floor on Days 46 and 58, and swimming on all four Tuesdays, Days 40, 47, 54 and 61. Kreston can drown in a dishpan's depth, so tip it out the moment she finishes and stay within arm's reach of both children at the pool.
- **February cold**: three walks go hunting frost and ice, Days 37, 38 and 46, and Day 58 puts her bare palms on cold metal. Take the salted side of the path, keep one hand free for her, and gloves go back on the moment she says cold.

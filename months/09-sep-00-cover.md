## ⚠️ Quick Safety Reminders

- **Standing and open water**: the Labor Day dishpans, the mud bucket on the 11th, the water run buckets on the 14th, the chalk bucket on the 23rd, the fence bucket on the 30th, the pond on the 19th and the 27th. Kreston can drown in two inches, so tip each container out as the activity ends and hold him at the pond and the pool.
- **Small parts**: the six wrapped parcels on the 3rd, the six objects buried in soil on the 11th, the dyed pasta on the 22nd, the buckeyes on the 23rd and the 26th, the letter cards on the 29th. He runs now and mouths what he reaches, so count them out and count them back in.
- **String and cord**: the mail bag strap on the 3rd, the lantern handle on the 5th, the pasta necklace on the 22nd, the jump rope curved into letters on the 25th, the three tied parcels on the 30th. A loop at his height is a strangulation risk, so take it down the moment the activity ends.
- **Curbs and traffic**: the driveway crossing on the 4th, the three curbside stops on the 15th, the chalk name path on the 24th, the street sign hunt on the 29th. Keep yourself between both children and the road, with Kreston on a hand or in the stroller with the brake on.
- **Adult-only steps**: the knife that carves the potato stamps on the 8th and the 22nd, the stapler on the finds book on the 15th, the sharp knife on the eating tile on the 20th, the craft knife on the throwing box on the 26th, adult scissors on most days, the oven on the 30th. Each one goes back to your hand or a high shelf between uses.
- **September specific**: the first two weeks run hot, so hat on and cream before the door opens, again at eleven. Wasps come for anything sweet outdoors on the 14th, and after the equinox on the 23rd the park light goes at seven.

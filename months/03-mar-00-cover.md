## ⚠️ Quick Safety Reminders

- **Seed-sized everything**: cress seed, soaked beans, loose rice, rubber bands, the trap's chocolate coins. Count them out, count them back, and sweep before he goes down.
- **Balloons**: Day 77 is catching day, and burst pieces are the worst choking hazard in the house for both children. Use the scarf instead any time Kreston is on the floor.
- **Anything he can pull up on**: the tent chairs all circus week, the paper bin, the laundry basket nest, the oven door. Weight it, push it to a wall, or lift the activity onto the table.
- **String and cord**: the tent flag line, the nest, the leprechaun trap and the sorting circles all use it. Take it down the moment the activity ends.
- **Adult-only steps**: scissors most days, the craft knife on mud kitchen day, the oven through forty minutes of soda bread. Scissors go back out of reach between cuts, not down on the table.
- **Water, and there is more of it this month**: the mud kitchen sink, the pond edge on the frog egg walk, the gutters running after March rain, swimming every Tuesday. That sink holds enough to drown Kreston, so tip it out every time you step away, and stay within arm's reach of both children at the pool.
- **Spring specific**: daffodil bulbs and yew are poisonous, and both grow along the route she collects on, so nothing she picks goes near a mouth. Wash hands after soil and after raw egg, and Easter Sunday's dyed eggs are for looking at.

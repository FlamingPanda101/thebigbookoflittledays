## 3. THE DAY FORMAT — 8:00 AM to 6:00 PM

Two-page spread. **1,000–1,150 words.** Morning on the left, afternoon on the
right. Print double-sided.

```
- **8:00–9:00 AM** — 🌅 Opening Activity: [NAMED — varies every day]
- **9:00–9:15 AM** — Morning Meeting (15 min)
- **9:15–10:15 AM** — 🎨 Main Event: [NAMED] (60 min)
- **10:15–10:30 AM** — Snack (15 min)
- **10:30–11:15 AM** — 🌳 Get Outside: [NAMED] (45 min)
- **11:15–11:45 AM** — Free Play (30 min)
- **11:45 AM–12:00 PM** — 🤸 Wiggle Time (15 min)
- **12:00–12:30 PM** — Lunch
- **12:30–1:00 PM** — 📚 Book Time
- **1:00–2:30 PM** — 🎨 Second Main Event: [NAMED] (90 min)
- **2:30–3:30 PM** — 🌳 Out Again: **Pick one** (60 min)
- **3:30–4:00 PM** — Snack & Free Play
- **4:00–4:15 PM** — 🧹 Her Job: [NAMED]
- **4:15–5:00 PM** — Quiet Play: [NAMED]
- **5:00–5:45 PM** — 🛁 Wind-Down: [NAMED]
- **6:00 PM** — **Dinner**
```

**🌙 Prep Tonight** — ONE line at the top of every day. What to get out,
defrost, freeze or set up the night before. Two main events a day makes this
load-bearing, not a nicety. Every day gets one.

**🌅 Opening Activity (8:00–9:00)** — named, varies every day, never a routine.
Gentle: she has just come to the table and the Main Event is an hour away.
It usually **preps or sets up the 9:15 Main Event** so the day builds instead
of starting earlier. Materials in one line, 2–3 steps, ~90 words.

**🎨 Main Event (9:15–10:15)** — full materials list with quantities, 4–6
numbered steps, one Tip. Self-contained. Never link out.

**🌳 Get Outside (10:30–11:15)** — a specific named activity, season-matched.
Never a generic "Park Trip."

**🎨 Second Main Event (1:00–2:30)** — a real 90-minute activity with its own
full materials list and 5–7 numbered steps, plus one Tip. Not filler and not a
repeat. It either **extends** the morning's work or **switches mode**:
morning made something → afternoon plays with it; morning was messy →
afternoon is calm.

**🎯 Afternoon Alternatives** — 3–4 one-line options for when it doesn't land.
Titles only, no steps.

**🌳 Out Again (2:30–3:30)** — pick one, 4–6 one-line options. See section 5.

**🧹 Her Job / Quiet Play / 🛁 Wind-Down** — named in the schedule block only.
They do not get their own prose sections. Her Job is one real household job,
tied to dinner where possible.

**🧠 A Little Parenting Insight** — 100–120 words, evidence-informed, written
to Brooklyn as a competent adult. Bold claim as a headline, then the content.
Framed as *why what she already does works*, never corrective. Never preachy.
Must not repeat an earlier insight.

**⚠️ Safety** — wherever there is a real hazard. Not on every day. No filler
warnings; they train the reader to skip them.

**🌍 Around the World** — ONE per week, attached to the week's first day.
Never daily. Format: fact + **✨ Try it** (food-forward) + *Did you know?*

- Tone: warm, practical, never preachy. Written to a competent adult.

## 8. BOOKLET STRUCTURE

Twelve printable booklets, split on whole weeks:

`Jan 1–5 · Feb 6–9 · Mar 10–13 · Apr 14–18 · May 19–22 · Jun 23–26 ·
Jul 27–31 · Aug 32–35 · Sep 36–39 · Oct 40–44 · Nov 45–48 · Dec 49–52 + Day 365`

Each booklet **opens** with:
1. `<a id="booklet-[month]"></a>` + cover: title, "Booklet N of 12", date range, dedication
2. **This Month** contents with links
3. Kreston's age note
4. **⚠️ Quick Safety Reminders** — season-specific

Each booklet **ends** with a backup section:
- `<a id="backup-[month]"></a>`
- **2 Bad Weather Days** — full day plans, written for *that month's* real weather
- **2 Sick Days** — no outdoor section, a rest block instead, vague schedules ("whenever she wakes")
- **1 Extra Ideas page** — 🎲 Alternative Activities and 🎨 Alternative Arts & Crafts, ~11 each, plus a "Kreston's Turn" entry
- Anchors: `[mon]-weather-1`, `[mon]-weather-2`, `[mon]-sick-1`, `[mon]-sick-2`, `[mon]-extras`
- Backup days carry insights too

## 9. NAVIGATION

Use **explicit HTML anchors**, never auto-generated heading IDs. Emoji in
headings break slug generation in most markdown→PDF converters.

- `<a id="day-N"></a>` before every day header
- `<a id="week-N"></a>` before every week header
- `<a id="booklet-month"></a>` before every booklet cover
- `<div style="page-break-after: always;"></div>` after every day and booklet
